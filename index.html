Crenzy does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to play the Game.

Crenzy offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Payments and transaction processing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card or other payment instrument details.

Crenzy may use Google Play Games services to provide game-related features such as saving or synchronizing game progress.

If you use Google Play Games services, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games account according to Google's privacy policies.

Playing Crenzy does not require you to create a separate Crenzy account.

Crenzy may use:

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Crenzy is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Retention

We do not maintain a separate user database containing personal information collected directly from players.

Information processed by third-party services is retained and managed according to the policies of those services.

You can stop using Crenzy at any time.

You may also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings.
